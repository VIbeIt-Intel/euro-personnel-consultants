Recruitment Process Overview
Our recruitment process is designed to efficiently match employers with suitable candidates. We ideally require one to two months’ head start to ensure smooth preparation.
Step 1: Employer Requirements
We work closely with employers to understand their specific needs, including:
•	Job specifications
•	Smoking or alcohol policies
•	Physical requirements
•	Skills and experience
•	Any additional employer specific criteria
Step 2: Candidate Search
We search our internal database for suitable candidates. If none are available, we advertise the position on:
•	Facebook (Euro Personnel Consultants)
•	TikTok (@europersonnel)

Step 3: Personal Screening
All applicants undergo a personal interview to ensure they meet the employer’s requirements as closely as possible.
Step 4: Resume Submission & Employer Interviews
We forward selected resumes to the employer, who may or must:
•	Review and shortlist candidates
•	Conduct interviews via WhatsApp or Zoom
•	Contact candidates directly
Step 5: Employment Offer & Visa Process
Once the employer confirms their selections, the offer email serves as the trigger for us to begin visa preparations
Optional: Some employers prefer to receive all resumes and make their own selections. We provide recommendations but respect employer discretion.
Communication
We maintain transparent communication throughout the process:
•	Informing unsuccessful candidates
•	Employers confirming final worker lists
•	Ensuring all parties remain updated at each stage
Consulate Processing Overview – Euro Personnel Consultants
1. Employer Submission of Worker List
Employers must provide a complete worker list including:
•	Full name
•	Contact number
•	Email address
This allows us to begin visa preparation immediately.

2. Initial Worker Contact & Application Forms
We contact each worker directly.
•	Employer recruited workers receive our Visa Application Form.
•	Euro Personnel recruited workers use the Recruitment Form completed during their interview.
3. WhatsApp Group Creation
All selected workers are added to a dedicated WhatsApp group for updates and guidance. Employers may join if they wish to monitor progress.
4. Completion of DS 160 Visa Applications
Once we receive the ETA 790 and I 797B, we finalize the already prepared DS 160 applications.
Workers only need to submit their completed forms. Euro Personnel handles:
•	Quality control
•	DS 160 completion
•	Visa fee payment
•	Appointment scheduling
This ensures accuracy and prevents delays.
5. Visa Fee Payment & Appointment Scheduling
After DS 160 completion:
•	Workers are contacted and the visa fee is paid together with Euro Personnel
•	Appointments are scheduled as soon as slots become available
While appointment availability is outside our control, we always secure the earliest possible dates.
6. Seasonal Deadlines & Preparation
To ensure smooth processing, all visa applications must be submitted before 20 December. This means:
•	Recruitment must be completed before 20 December
•	Worker lists must be finalized
Even if workers only report in March, we aim to have all applications ready 1–2 months beforehand, allowing immediate action once the ETA 790 and I 797B are issued.
7. Ongoing Communication
We maintain constant communication with employers and workers through:
•	WhatsApp groups
•	Direct messaging
•	Email updates
This ensures transparency, quick responses, and efficient coordination throughout the consulate process.
