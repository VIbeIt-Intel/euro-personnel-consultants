What we do

🌍 Euro Personnel Consultants – Company Profile Established: 1999 Location: South Africa Specialization: H 2A Agricultural Recruitment (USA & Ireland) 🏛 About Us Euro Personnel Consultants is South Africa’s oldest and most trusted H 2A recruitment consultancy, proudly serving the agricultural sector since 1999. With over 27 years of experience, we have successfully placed more than 5,000 agricultural workers in the United States and Ireland, building a reputation rooted in integrity, loyalty, and excellence. 💼 Our Approach We believe in doing things the right way, the old school way. Every candidate undergoes a personal, interview before being introduced to our valued employers. This hands on approach ensures precise matching and contributes to our 98% annual return rate. We don’t sugarcoat or take shortcuts. We tell it like it is, and our results speak for themselves. 🤝 Trusted Partnerships We are deeply grateful to our U.S. agents for their continued support and are proud to hold an exclusive contract with one of the largest H 2A agents in the United States. Our commitment is to provide them, and our employers, with exceptional service, every single time. Not neglecting our other valuable agents, we strive to provide each of them with the same excellent service they have become accustomed to over the past 27 years. Our commitment is to deliver consistent, reliable, and exceptional support to all partners, every single time. 👩‍💼 Leadership & Legacy Our founding member, Magda van der Westhuizen, built an extraordinary legacy since 1999. Known for her unwavering integrity and diligence, she earned the trust of employers and workers alike. In 2025, she passed the reins to her daughter, Charlotte Portwig, an ex soldier, ensuring that the values of loyalty, honesty, and excellence continue to guide our mission.
